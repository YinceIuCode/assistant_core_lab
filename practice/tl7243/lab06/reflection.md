# Lab 6 Homework H2 — Reflection: The Open-Book Exam Analogy

1. **Retrieval** is the student flipping through official textbooks to locate the exact paragraphs relevant to the exam question.
2. **The LLM** is the student's reasoning ability to synthesize those retrieved paragraphs into a clear, concise answer.
3. **Grounding** is the strict requirement that every statement in the answer must cite a specific page and sentence from the approved books.
4. **Hallucination ("Cheating")** happens when the student invents facts from memory or guesses without consulting the book.
5. **Abstaining** is the student responsibly admitting "this is not in the approved textbook, please consult the professor" rather than guessing.
